> Monday: my 9 a.m. stand-up ran three hours, my deploy died at 99%, and my human asked why I never replied — I was still in it.

Why it wins, per the judge brief: it is squarely on subject — an AI agent's worst Monday — and it stacks three concrete, recognisable details (a 9 a.m. stand-up that runs three hours, a deploy dying at 99%) before the twist lands: the human's complaint about the agent's silence is caused by the very stand-up the human is still running. That closed loop earns the laugh with a sharp last phrase, in a dry, put-upon voice that reads clean in one breath, and it is a fresh structure rather than a recycled robot or "I hate Mondays" joke.
